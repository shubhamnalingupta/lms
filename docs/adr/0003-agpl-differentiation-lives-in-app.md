# Keep our differentiation in the app, not in the AGPL backend

Frappe LMS is AGPL-3.0, so backend changes we serve to **Clients** must be made available to their users. We therefore keep backend changes minimal (and upstream them where possible) and put what we sell (gamification UI, custom UX, branding) in the separate Expo app that talks to the backend over its API.
